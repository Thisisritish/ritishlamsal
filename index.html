The Warrior's Compass

A personal rulebook for focus, equanimity, and purposeful action. Not perfection. Direction.

कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन।
"You have the right to action alone, never to its fruits." — Bhagavad Gita 2.47
↓

Foundational Truths

Your inner ground. Return to these when the mind clouds or the path feels heavy.

1. Doer, Not Owner

Act with full effort. Release attachment to outcome. Success and failure are teachers, not judges.

2. Dharma is Anchor

In confusion, ask: "What is the righteous action before me—right now?" Not what is easy. What is true.

3. Mind: Friend & Foe

Train it with discipline. Guard it with discernment. When it races, return to the breath. When it doubts, return to purpose.

4. Equanimity is Strength

Praise and criticism, gain and loss, pleasure and pain—meet them all with steady wisdom. This is Sthitaprajna.

5. You Are the Witness

You are not your thoughts. Observe fear, doubt, desire. Do not fight them. Do not follow them. See them. Let them pass. You remain.

The Three Pillars

Paths of practice. Weave them into your daily rhythm.

🕉️ Karma Yoga — Right Action

  • Act without anxiety. Do the work because it is yours.
  • One-pointed focus. Multitasking is fragmentation.
  • Start before you are ready. Begin. Adjust. Continue.
  • Effort is your offering. Measure sincerity, not metrics.
  • When stuck: "What is the next smallest right action?"

🧠 Jnana Yoga — Wisdom & Discernment

  • Separate signal from noise. Focus on what you control.
  • Question narratives. Replace "I am behind" with "I am becoming."
  • Seek knowledge, not just information. Let wisdom compound.
  • Doubt is a tool, not a trap. Use it to inquire.
  • Write to clarify. Journaling is mental surgery.

🧘 Dhyana Yoga — Inner Discipline

  • Guard your attention like sacred space. Curate inputs.
  • Create mental silence daily. Stillness rewires reactivity.
  • When overwhelmed, return to the breath. Anchor in the present.
  • Rest is renewal. Burnout is imbalance.
  • Speak to yourself as Krishna would to Arjuna: with compassion and unwavering belief.

Rules for the Journey

Practical boundaries for mind, growth, relationships, and self.

🛡️ Mental Hygiene

  • Do not feed the "what-if" monster. Worry is imagination misused.
  • Comparison is theft. Your journey is yours alone.
  • When fear arises, name it. Naming reduces its power.
  • Practice "mental boxing": Acknowledge distractions, place them in a box, return to focus.
  • End each day with: "Did I act with integrity?"

🌱 Growth & Learning

  • Foundations before fireworks. Master the basics.
  • Teach to learn. Explain concepts in your own words.
  • Seek friction, not comfort. Growth lives at the edge of understanding.
  • Feedback is fuel. Extract the lesson. Discard the noise.
  • Your brand is your character in action. Let consistency speak.

🤝 Relationships & Environment

  • Choose influences wisely. Spend time with those who elevate.
  • Serve without expectation. This is Karma Yoga in community.
  • Set boundaries with love. "No" to dilution is "yes" to Dharma.
  • Honor your roots. Carry identity with pride.
  • When in doubt, withdraw to reflect. Solitude is recalibration.

💙 Self & Compassion

  • You are not your productivity. Your worth is inherent.
  • Speak to yourself in the voice of your highest self.
  • Honor the body. Move it. Nourish it. Rest it.
  • Forgive yourself quickly. Mistakes are data. Regret leads to adjustment, then release.
  • Remember: you are both student and teacher. Stay humble. Stay curious.

The Practice

How to live this rulebook without rigidity.

🌅 Today's Intention

Choose one principle to embody. Save it. Revisit tonight.

📖 Daily Rhythm

Morning (2 min): Read one principle. Set one intention.
Evening (5 min): Journal: Where did I act with focus? Where did I lose equanimity? What to adjust?
Weekly (15 min): Re-read. What resonated? What needs refinement?

🆘 Crisis Protocol

When overwhelmed, panic, or lost in noise.

"I am not the body, nor the mind.
I am the conscious witness, the eternal learner, the humble doer.
I act with sincerity. I release the fruits.
I walk my Dharma—one step, one breath, one day at a time."

🕉️ Crisis Protocol

  1. Pause. Inhale 4s, hold 4s, exhale 6s. Repeat 3x.
  2. Name it. "This is overwhelm / fear / doubt. It is temporary."
  3. Ask: "What is within my control right now?"
  4. Do that one thing. Smallest right action. Nothing else.
  5. Return to breath. Anchor in the present. You are safe. You are capable.

Close this when ready. The storm passes. You remain.
